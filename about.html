About Rainbow Rights South Africa

Rainbow Rights South Africa is committed to promoting equality, inclusion and dignity for LGBTQIA+ communities throughout South Africa.

Who We Are

Rainbow Rights South Africa is a non-profit organisation committed to creating a society free from discrimination and inequality.

The organisation works with communities, educational institutions and support organisations to advocate for human rights and equal opportunities.

Mission

To improve human rights, equality and dignity for LGBTQIA+ people through education, advocacy, support services and community involvement.

Vision

A South Africa free from prejudice, discrimination and inequality where every individual is treated with dignity and respect.

Our Core Values

Community

Building strong and supportive communities.

Support

Providing guidance and access to support services.

Human Rights

Promoting equality, dignity and legal rights for all.
