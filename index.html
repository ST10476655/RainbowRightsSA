About Us Summary

Who Are We?

Rainbow Rights South Africa advocates for equality, dignity, human rights and inclusion. We provide educational resources, support services and awareness campaigns that help create safer and more inclusive communities.

Mission Statement

To improve human rights, equality and dignity for LGBTQIA+ people through community involvement, education, support services and advocacy.

Vision Statement

A South Africa free from prejudice and discrimination where all LGBTQIA+ people are treated equally and with dignity.
